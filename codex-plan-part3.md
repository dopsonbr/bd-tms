# Codex Plan Part 3: AI, Voice, and Demo Finalization

## Stage Goal
- Complete the AI and voice simulation layer and polish the POC into a guided, reliable demo artifact with deterministic scenario handling.

## Scope
- AI chat interface with context-aware simulated responses.
- Recommendation confidence and reasoning panel behavior.
- Voice dashboard and immersive call playback simulation.
- Planning assistant and tomorrow plan review interactions.
- Demo controls completion: scenario selector, time simulator, event injector, guided tour, reset flows.
- End-to-end Scenario D and E support.

## Out Of Scope
- Production-ready external telephony and speech services.
- Real-time backend persistence.
