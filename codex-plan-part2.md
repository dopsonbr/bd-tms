# Codex Plan Part 2: Core Operations Workflows

## Stage Goal
- Implement high-value carrier and brokerage workflows over the Stage 1 foundation, including dispatch interaction, load lifecycle behavior, and operational analytics.

## Scope
- Dashboard data-rich experience.
- Loads list, detail, lifecycle, and dispatch interactions.
- Fleet map/list shell with truck and driver summaries.
- Brokerage load board and carrier matching behavior.
- Reporting dashboard with KPI and trend visualizations.
- End-to-end Scenario A, B, and C support.

## Out Of Scope
- Full AI natural-language engine behavior beyond guided recommendations.
- Full voice call immersive playback.

## Workflow Acceptance Criteria
- Load assignment updates dashboard, load detail, and fleet surfaces in one state transition.
- Exception actions update feed severity and timeline outcomes.
- Brokerage matching calculates and displays margin impact.
- KPI cards and report charts update when scenario or time changes.
- Scenarios A/B/C are triggerable from demo controls and complete with expected state transitions.
